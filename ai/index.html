AI Academy Atlas

Almost every major AI company, cloud provider and university now teaches AI for free, but the choices can feel like a maze. The AI Academy Atlas is your map: free AI learning from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Microsoft, IBM, AWS, NVIDIA, Hugging Face, DeepLearning.AI, Harvard, MIT, Stanford and more, each with an honest cost label so you always know what is free, what earns a certificate and what is not.

Whether you are eight or eighty, a parent, a teacher, a business owner or a future builder, there is a good first step for you. Start with a path that fits you, or browse the universities one by one. Everything we recommend can be completed for $0, and where a certificate costs money we say so plainly.

AI Academy Atlas FAQ

Is everything in the Atlas really free?

Every path we recommend can be finished for $0. Some providers also sell paid plans or paid certificates. When that is the case, the card says so, for example “Free to audit · paid certificate”.

Who issues the certificates and badges?

The providers do, such as Anthropic Academy, IBM SkillsBuild, Hugging Face and Salesforce Trailhead. Ignite Curiosity picks, orders and explains the courses. We never issue or sell the credentials.

Where should a complete beginner start?

Open the Learning Paths page and choose the path for your situation. Elements of AI and the Anthropic Academy beginner courses are friendly first steps.

Is there something for kids and teens?

Yes. The Learning Paths page has a kids-and-teens path with hands-on, no-code activities, and a path for educators and families.

How do Curiosity Coaches use the Atlas?

A coach picks a path with the learner, agrees a four-week plan and uses discussion prompts from the Coaching Kit on the Learning Paths page.

How current is it?

The Atlas was checked in 2026. AI courses change quickly, so always confirm cost and certificate terms on the official page before you start.
