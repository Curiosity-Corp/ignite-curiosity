QUBIT Quest: Quantum for everyone

Quantum computers use the strange rules of physics to solve problems that are very hard for ordinary computers, and the world will need curious people to program them, secure them and apply them. QUBIT Quest is a free learning track that takes you from your first big idea about qubits to a portfolio worthy of a quantum developer. Every resource is free to start, and we flag the few places where a certificate or exam costs money.

No physics degree is needed to begin. The track has 4 pages, five pillars and a free 6 to 12 month roadmap, and your Curiosity Coach can adapt it for any age or level.

QUBIT Quest FAQ

Do I need a physics degree?

No. QUBIT Quest starts with plain-language ideas and builds the math slowly. If you can follow a good video and try a simulator, you can begin.

Is it really free?

Yes, every step on the roadmap can be completed for $0. A few providers charge for badge exams or certificates, and the pages label those clearly as optional.

What age is it for?

Curious learners from about 12 up can use the foundations and simulators, and the Foundations page lists programs for younger students. Coaches adapt the pace to the learner.

Can I run real quantum programs?

Yes. The Programming page shows free ways to run circuits in a simulator and on real quantum hardware through providers' free tiers. Check each provider's current terms.

Will I get a job as a quantum developer?

The roadmap builds the skills and a portfolio that employers look for, but nobody can promise a job. The Careers page is honest about roles, certifications and costs.

How do Curiosity Coaches help?

A coach agrees a plan with you, answers the “why” questions and uses the coaching prompts and 4-week plans on each page. Book a coach with the button on this page.
