Transform Any Room into a Learning Lab

Available Worldwide Your School, Library, Community Center, or Home

"Curiosity is the spark behind every great discovery, every breakthrough, every new idea that changes the world."

SPARK Spaces are dedicated learning environments designed to ignite curiosity and hands-on exploration without requiring an entire house. Unlike our full Curiosity Cottages, SPARK Spaces are rooms or sections of rooms that contain a carefully curated selection of exploration tools. They're perfect for schools, libraries, community centers, or even a corner of your home.

Each SPARK Space is thoughtfully designed with accessible, engaging tools that encourage learners of all ages to explore STEAM subjects through hands-on discovery. Whether you're an educator looking to enhance your classroom, a librarian creating an interactive learning area, or a parent wanting to inspire curiosity at home, SPARK Spaces provide an achievable pathway to experiential learning.

What Makes a SPARK Space?

SPARK Spaces are characterized by their flexibility and accessibility. Unlike full learning houses, SPARK Spaces focus on creating impactful learning experiences within a smaller footprint:

  • Room-based or sectioned areas: Not full houses, but dedicated spaces within existing structures
  • Carefully selected tools: A few high-impact exploration tools rather than extensive collections
  • Quick access to discovery: Immediate engagement without overwhelming setup requirements
  • Scalable learning: Start small and expand as interest and resources grow
  • Multiple settings: Perfect for classrooms, library corners, community rooms, or home offices

Setting Up Your SPARK Space

Creating a SPARK Space is straightforward and can be tailored to your specific needs, budget, and available area. Here's how to get started:

  1. Choose Your Location: Identify a room or section that can be dedicated to exploration - it doesn't need to be large, just accessible and inviting.
  2. Select Your Focus: Decide which areas of STEAM learning align with your community's interests - robotics, art, coding, engineering, or a combination.
  3. Curate Your Tools: Choose a few high-quality exploration tools that encourage hands-on learning. Quality over quantity is key.
  4. Create an Inviting Environment: Make the space welcoming with comfortable seating, good lighting, and clear organization.
  5. Establish Guidelines: Set up simple rules for tool usage, safety, and collaboration to ensure everyone can explore freely.

Who Are SPARK Spaces For?

SPARK Spaces serve diverse learning communities across multiple settings:

  • Schools and Classrooms: Teachers creating maker spaces or STEAM labs within their classrooms
  • Public Libraries: Librarians offering hands-on learning alongside traditional resources
  • Community Centers: Organizations providing accessible STEAM education to underserved communities
  • Homeschool Families: Parents fostering curiosity-driven learning at home
  • After-School Programs: Youth organizations expanding their educational offerings
  • Corporate Learning Centers: Businesses creating innovation spaces for employee development

Ready for More?

If you love the concept of SPARK Spaces but are ready for a fully immersive, house-sized learning experience, explore our Curiosity Cottages. Curiosity Cottages take the SPARK Space concept to the next level - entire homes transformed into peaceful learning centers where curiosity guides exploration across every room.

While SPARK Spaces provide focused, accessible learning in a single room or area, Curiosity Cottages offer a complete residential learning environment. Both serve the same mission: igniting curiosity and making STEAM education engaging and accessible for learners of all ages.

About SPARK Spaces

SPARK Spaces make hands-on STEAM learning accessible to everyone. By transforming a single room or section into a discovery zone, we're removing barriers to curiosity-driven education.

These spaces empower educators, librarians, and parents to create meaningful learning experiences without the need for extensive facilities or budgets. Start small, dream big, and watch curiosity flourish.
