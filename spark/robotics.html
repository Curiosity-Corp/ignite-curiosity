A Core Focus

Robotics is a core focus of Ignite Curiosity: machines that work beside people. A robot that carries, sorts or guides has to sense the world, decide what to do and stop when a person is in the way. You learn to build one, program it and prove that it does its job safely.

The robotics club path below is an offering we are preparing. It is written here so you can see what you will do, and so mentors can help us shape it.

The Robotics Club Path with a SPARK

You build a robot, you program it, and you take it into a challenge. The SPARK Learning System™ (Seek, Play, Apply, Research, Know) sets the order, and each step ends with a check you can show.

S

Seek

Pick one job a robot could do beside people: carry a load, sort objects, guide someone to a door. Write down what the robot must do and what it must never do.
Check: a one-page job card your club agrees on.
P

Play: Build

Put a simple robot together, or build one in a simulator. Try the motors, the wheels and the sensors one at a time.
Check: your robot drives, turns and stops on command.
A

Apply: Program

Write the code that lets it sense and react: follow a line, keep its distance from an obstacle, pick up an object and put it down.
Check: it finishes your job card’s task three times in a row, and stops every time something blocks its path.
R

Research: Compete

Take your robot into a club challenge. Watch the other teams’ robots, find where yours failed and why, and fix one thing.
Check: a short report: what worked, what failed, what you changed.
K

Know

Show a newer club member how your robot works and help them get theirs moving.
Check: their robot passes the Play check with your help.

Steps move on when the check is passed, not by the calendar.
