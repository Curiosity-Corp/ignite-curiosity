Who It Is For

The Candidate Placement Internship is an Ignite Curiosity internship. It fits under the SPARK Learning System™ and is taught with the SMILE Method.

It is for students and early starters who want to learn recruiting, career placement and business development. A second-year student on a BBA or a related degree is a typical fit.

No recruiting experience is needed. What you bring: curiosity, clear written English, honesty and discretion.

The work happens remotely in Dan Hup, the placement department, in its web dashboard.

What You Learn with a SPARK

The SPARK Learning System™ is our Seek, Play, Apply, Research, Know approach. Through it, students go through a 5-step process to encourage themselves, ignite their own curiosity, find inner passions, collaborate with like-minded individuals who share similar goals and who are discovering the same subjects, and finally to engage in community development to share wisdom back with those who need it most.

S

Seek

Orientation (SPARK Orientation, then the Dan Hup dashboard), reading a candidate brief, and confidentiality.
Check: five practice roles logged, a brief review with your mentor, and a scenario review.
P

Play

Sourcing and research on practice cases, and outreach writing for practice cases.
Check: ten roles reviewed for fit, and five drafts approved with light edits.
A

Apply

Record discipline on the live pipeline, and interview and training preparation for a real candidate.
Check: a weekly record review with no gaps, and one full prep pack reviewed.
R

Research

AI tools, verified, and going deeper in the Human Resources and Sales & Growth tracks.
Check: a checked role summary, and a note on one thing to change.
K

Know

Shadow, then supervised, then independent; and teach one part to a newer intern.
Check: your mentor signs off each skill.

Stages move on when the check is passed, not by the calendar.

How We Use SMILE

SMILE: a Self-Motivated Impact Learning Environment. SPARK is how you learn each skill; SMILE is the environment you learn it in:

  • Self-motivated: you are responsible for your own exploration; you set weekly goals and write the Friday note.
  • Impact: the work has real results for real people: candidates’ careers.
  • Learning environment: your mentor does not hold your hand; mistakes are valuable; learning is tied to work that matters.

Tracks It Draws On

The SPARK tracks you take:

  • SPARK Orientation
  • Soft Skills
  • Human Resources
  • Sales & Growth Hacking
  • Business Development

The Path Onward

Stage 1 is complete when your mentor has signed off every skill (the Know phase), and you have taught one part of the work to a newer intern and written your own placement playbook.

Interns who complete it can later apply for a US Fellows fellowship through the Career Advancement Track. Stage 2 is the Education Fellows: Career Mentorship track, a named track inside the existing US Fellows Education program (“Learning, youth opportunity, mentorship, and human potential”). Its work is helping people advance their careers, including mentoring new interns.

Moving on takes a completed internship, the placement playbook, and the field and record US Fellows’ own criteria require, usually after graduation. US Fellows makes its own selection; the internship does not guarantee a fellowship. Read about the Education Fellows: Career Mentorship track on usfellows.org.
