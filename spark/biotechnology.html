A Core Focus

Biotechnology is a core focus of Ignite Curiosity: biology engineered responsibly. Living things already clean water, turn waste into food and make medicines. Biotechnology learns from them and designs with them, and asks at every step whether a design is safe and fair.

The biodesign challenge path below is an offering we are preparing. It is written here so you can see what you will do, and so mentors can help us shape it.

The Biodesign Challenge Path with a SPARK

You pose a problem, you design an answer, and you present it. This is a design challenge: you work with ideas, public data and a computer, and you need no lab. The SPARK Learning System™ (Seek, Play, Apply, Research, Know) sets the order, and each step ends with a check you can show.

S

Seek: Pose a Problem

Find a real problem near you that biology might help with: polluted water, food that goes to waste, plastic that does not break down.
Check: a one-paragraph problem statement that says who the problem hurts.
P

Play

Look at how living things already deal with problems like yours, in nature and in published research.
Check: three examples, each with the source you found it in.
A

Apply: Design

Sketch your answer: what it is, how it would work and who would use it. Draw it, model it, or explore public biological data on a computer.
Check: a design sheet a mentor has reviewed.
R

Research: Responsibly

Ask what could go wrong: for people, for other living things, for the place it would be used. Change the design until you have an answer for each risk.
Check: a list of risks and what your design does about each one.
K

Know: Present

Present your design to mentors and other challengers, answer their questions, and help a newer challenger pose their first problem.
Check: your presentation, and your written answers to the questions it raised.

Steps move on when the check is passed, not by the calendar.

Training Track

Two biotechnology training tracks are on DeveloperDojo: bioinformatics (reading and analysing biological data with code) and lab automation in code (planning experiments in software and running them in a simulator, starting with biosafety).

Open a track to start the training, or browse every track in the DeveloperDojo track library.

Mentor a Biodesign Challenger

Scientists, engineers, health workers and teachers: help young people turn a problem they care about into a design they can defend. Tell us what you can help with (biology, data, safety and ethics, or presenting) and we will contact you as the challenge path takes shape.
