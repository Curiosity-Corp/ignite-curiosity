Choose Your Pathway

Whether you're here to discover your spark or help others find theirs, there's a place for you in our community.

I Want to Learn

No commitment. No pressure. Just exploration.

Explore activities and discover what ignites your passion. Whether you're a student, career changer, retiree, or lifelong learner of any age, embark on your SPARK journey of self-discovery and growth. You don't have to know what you want yet - that's the whole point!

I Want to Volunteer/Coach

Share your knowledge and help others on their spark journey. Guide learners of all ages as they explore, discover, and grow into who they're meant to be.

Your SPARK Journey

A lifelong learning adventure designed for curious minds of all ages.

Remember: No commitment required. Try lots of activities. Switch paths. Explore freely. Discovery comes through experimentation, not commitment.

01

Explore & Discover

Browse spark areas and try lots of activities. Try different SPARK activities across various fields - from technology to arts, business to creativity. Experiment freely to find what truly excites you. This is your time to explore without pressure or judgment.
02

Attend SPARK Sessions

Dive deeper into what interests you. When something clicks, dive deeper. Attend SPARK sessions, join camps, and connect with coaches who can guide your journey. Whether you're 8 or 80, it's never too late to discover your passion.
03

Join the Community

Connect with fellow curious learners. Connect with fellow learners in the Curiosity Hive. Share your discoveries, learn from others, and celebrate the joy of continuous growth and exploration.
04

Experience Curiosity Camps

Immersive experiences to accelerate growth. Attend our signature camps where creativity meets mindful learning. Experience what it means to be fully immersed in subjects you love.
05

Graduate Knowing Yourself

Understand who you are and what ignites you. Graduate knowing yourself better than ever before. You'll understand your passions, strengths, and unique voice.
06

Stay Forever Curious

Continue learning at any age—curiosity never retires. Continue as a lifelong learner, always curious, always growing - and when you're ready, help others find their spark too.

How To Volunteer

Help learners of all ages discover their spark and unlock their potential through guidance and support.
01

Complete Orientation

Learn about the SPARK Learning System and understand how to guide learners on their journey of self-discovery. Become tech-savvy, passionate for learning and teaching, and build your Curiosity Score by implementing SPARK yourself.
02

Join A Project

After understanding our approach, announce your desire to volunteer in the Curiosity Community. Connect with Project Managers and join ongoing initiatives helping learners explore, discover, and grow.
03

Share Our Mission

Spread the word about lifelong learning and self-discovery. Help us reach students, career changers, retirees, and curious minds everywhere who are searching for their spark.
