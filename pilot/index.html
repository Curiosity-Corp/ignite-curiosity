PILOT Program™ Agentic Product Management

Product managers in 2026 do not just manage people and plans. They direct AI agents too. PILOT is a free learning track for product and project managers who run their work in GitLab: learn to think like a product manager, work in small loops, care about the customer experience, brief and review AI agents, and master the tools. Every course is curiosity-first, practical and free to complete.

PILOT sits alongside SPARK, SMILE and SWARM in the Ignite Curiosity family. It has 10 courses, a 12-week suggested path, and a free certification tracker so you can see exactly which credentials cost $0. No coding is needed, and you practise on the GitLab board you already have.

Your recommended 12-week path

This order builds skills in a sensible sequence: first learn to work with an AI teammate, then learn the job, then the rhythm, the tool, the customer, the craft, and finally the agents and the measuring. If you do every optional step in every course, the hours add up to about 203, so plan on roughly 17 hours a week. The first free credential in each course takes much less. Your coach can trim the plan with you.

GitLab is your PM workspace

The PILOT capstone is simple and real: your learning plan lives in the same GitLab workspace where you run your work. There is nothing new to set up, so please do not create a new project. Use the one you already have.

1. Log every step as a to-do

For each step in a course, open an issue on your board with the label learning::pilot. Add a short note about what you plan to learn, and close the issue when you finish the step. You can also add it to your GitLab To-Do list so it shows up in your daily view.

2. Plan it like a real project

Our GitLab is Ultimate, so you can use everything: group your course issues under an epic called “PILOT Program”, give each course a milestone or iteration, and watch the roadmap show your 12 weeks. That is product management practised on your own learning.

3. Keep your evidence

When you finish a step, paste the completion link or a screenshot of your certificate or badge into the issue. If a course does not issue a certificate, a screenshot of your completion is your proof. Your board becomes your portfolio.

4. Show it to a coach

At the end, book a coach and walk through your board together: what you learned, what you shipped, and what comes next. Bring an AI agent into the review if you like, and tell us what it got right and wrong.

PILOT Program FAQ

Who is the PILOT Program for?

Product and project managers, especially junior ones, who run their work in GitLab and want to work well with AI agents. You do not need to write code and you do not need access to code.

Does it cost anything?

No. Every course has a path you can finish for $0, and the certificates and badges in the free tracker cost nothing. Paid exams are listed only as an optional step for later, when a sponsor can cover them.

Who issues the certificates?

The providers do (Anthropic Academy, Hugging Face, IBM SkillsBuild and others). Ignite Curiosity picks and orders the courses, coaches you through them and checks that each claim on our pages is stated carefully. Where a course does not state a certificate, we say so.

Do I have to follow the 12-week order?

No. It is a suggestion that builds skills in a sensible order. Start with whichever course matches the work in front of you, and ask a coach to adjust the plan.

Do I need to create a new GitLab project?

No. Use the project and workspace you already have. Each course shows you what to practise on your real board, and you log each step there as a to-do.

How do I get help?

Book a coach with the button on this page. Bring your GitLab board and we will go through it together.
